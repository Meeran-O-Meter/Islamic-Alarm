## Welcome to...Meeran builds herself an alarm clock!
Now, why not buy a simple alarm that will be way more polished and cleaner than what I can make? Good question, you can have a headpat. <br><br>
Islamic prayer times change based on sun coordinates; hence changing everyday as the season does. I figured this would be a good time to use up some old electronic parts and have fun learning something new.

## Here's how it'll work.
First up, materials:
<ol>
  <li>Raspberry Pico 2W zero; conveniently at my disposal thanks to Flavortown.</li> <br>
  <li>2x16 LCD Display without I2C backpack; thanks to the old Arduino kit I was gifted. </li>
  <li>0.8W Speaker; collected from god knows where. </li>
  <li>4 push buttons</li>
  <li>Potentiometer</li>
  <li>IRF520 Transistor</li>
  <li>Resistors</li>

  For testing stages, I used a full sized breadboard, and an arduino uno.
</ol>
